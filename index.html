ISLAND PLANNING NOTE

Saint Martin's needs a little more planning.

It is an island trip, so the sea crossing depends on approved vessel operations, weather, tides and current government/operator rules. Build the trip around the vessel timetable rather than assuming a boat is available every day.

Once on the island, many places can be explored on foot. Snorkeling, diving and Chhera Dwip trips should be arranged through authorized/local operators and should follow current environmental and access rules.

More than sightseeing

Meet Bangladesh through culture.

Travel here is also food, weaving, music, architecture, riverside life and seasonal celebrations. Respect local communities, dress appropriately at religious sites and buy crafts directly from makers where possible.

01 · CRAFT

Jamdani

Fine handwoven textile tradition associated especially with the Dhaka region.

02 · FOOD

Flavours

Hilsa, bhorta, biryani, pitha and regional sweets turn a journey into a tasting trail.

03 · RIVER

Life on water

Launches, ferries, fishing boats and river markets reveal a side of Bangladesh that roads cannot.

04 · HERITAGE

Living history

Mosques, temples, monasteries, colonial buildings and archaeological landscapes span centuries.
